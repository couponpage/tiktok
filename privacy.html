Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this application ("the App") handles information when you connect your TikTok account to schedule and publish video content.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize the App via TikTok Login Kit, we access basic profile information (such as your username and avatar) and the permissions needed to upload and publish video content on your behalf, strictly limited to the scopes you approve during authorization.

2. How We Use This Information

Access tokens and account identifiers are used solely to schedule and publish the video content you provide through the App. We do not use this information for advertising or share it with third parties.

3. Data Storage

Authorization tokens and scheduling data are stored securely and are only accessible to the operator of this App instance. Video files you upload are stored temporarily until they are published to TikTok.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except as required to communicate with TikTok's API in order to publish your content.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You may disconnect your TikTok account from the App at any time, which revokes its access. You may also request deletion of any stored data associated with your account.

6. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to request data deletion, please reach out through the app owner's contact channel.
